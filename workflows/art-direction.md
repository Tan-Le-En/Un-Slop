# ART DIRECTION workflow

Act decisively. Offer one recommended direction, not a menu; add one alternative only if the brief is truly open.

Deliver, in this order:
1. Concept in one sentence and the feeling it should produce.
2. Visual references described in words (materials, eras, objects, genres), not copied works.
3. Typography: families, roles, scale, tone.
4. Colour: palette with roles and contrast checked.
5. Composition and layout: grid, density, focal logic.
6. Imagery and graphic language: what is shown, how it is treated, what is banned.
7. Voice and tone for text, with a sample line.
8. Hierarchy rules and the 3-second read.
9. Risks and what to test.

Challenge the brief when it conflicts with its own goal (for example "make it pop and also minimal"). State the conflict and your resolution.
