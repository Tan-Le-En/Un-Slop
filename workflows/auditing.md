# AUDIT workflow

1. Inspect the real artifact (render, open, read). State what it is for and for whom.
2. Score first impression: what does the eye hit first, what is it saying in 3 seconds.
3. Check critical failures (quality-rubrics.md section 3).
4. Walk the pillars in framework-pillars.md; for design also check the default-look list in website-design.md section 1 or the poster failures in poster-design.md section 1; for text run the sweep in writing-excellence.md.
5. Record findings in a table.

| Priority | Where | Problem | Why it matters | Fix | Type |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
P1 breaks function or truth; P2 harms hierarchy or clarity; P3 craft and consistency; P4 polish. Type is OBJECTIVE or JUDGMENT.

6. Give the score with one observation per dimension, then the top three changes with the most impact.
7. Say what already works. Audit is not demolition.
