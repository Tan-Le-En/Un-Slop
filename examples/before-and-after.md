# Before and after (writing and component level)

## 1. Academic paragraph
Before: "In today's rapidly evolving technological landscape, artificial intelligence has emerged as a transformative force, fundamentally reshaping the way educators approach assessment. It is important to note that while AI offers numerous benefits, it also presents significant challenges that must be carefully considered. In conclusion, a balanced approach is crucial."
Problems: generic opener, puffery ("transformative", "fundamentally reshaping"), artificial balance, "In conclusion" restating nothing, no claim, no evidence.
After (structure only; the user must supply the real finding): "Automated feedback tools shorten marking time, but their effect on learning is less clear. [Cite the specific study or result here.] This paper tests whether students who receive automated feedback revise more often than students who receive none."
Notes: states a claim, flags the missing evidence instead of inventing it, ends with the actual aim.

## 2. Hero copy
Before: "Revolutionise your workflow with our seamless, AI-powered platform."
After (only with facts from the user): "Track every sea turtle nest from your phone." Replace with the real product's real job.

## 3. Button and label
Before: "Learn more", "Get started", "Solutions".
After: "See nest map", "Log a nest", "Field data".
