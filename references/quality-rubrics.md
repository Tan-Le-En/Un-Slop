# Quality rubrics

Contents: 1 universal score, 2 domain weights, 3 critical failures, 4 score bands, 5 how to score, 6 self-critique, 7 revision loop.

## 1. Universal 100-point score
| Dimension | Pts | Question |
|---|---|---|
| Originality | 10 | Does it avoid generic templates and fit this brief specifically? |
| Typography | 10 | Is type chosen, scaled and set with intent? |
| Composition | 10 | Is the arrangement balanced, with clear grouping and alignment? |
| Hierarchy | 10 | Is the most important thing obvious in 3 seconds? |
| Clarity | 10 | Is the message precise and fast to grasp? |
| Authenticity | 10 | Is it free of default phrasing, stock visuals and fake detail? |
| Consistency | 10 | Do all elements obey one system? |
| Craftsmanship | 10 | Are details exact and finished? |
| Functionality | 10 | Does everything work, including accessibility and responsiveness? |
| Context | 10 | Does it suit audience, purpose, medium and constraints? |

Anchors per dimension: 9-10 exceptional with evidence; 7-8 solid, minor flaws; 5-6 acceptable but generic or uneven; 3-4 clear problems; 0-2 failing.

## 2. Domain emphasis
Adjust by shifting up to 10 points between dimensions, and record the shift.
- Writing: Clarity, Authenticity, Context up. Composition and Typography replaced by Structure (organisation) and Accuracy (facts match sources).
- Website: Functionality, Hierarchy, Typography, Consistency up.
- Poster or infographic: Hierarchy, Composition, Clarity up; add Data integrity.
- Slides: Clarity, Hierarchy, Consistency up.
- Brand identity: Originality, Consistency, Context up; add Versatility (works small, mono, in context).

## 3. Critical failures (cap the score)
Any one caps the total at 69 until fixed:
- Invented facts, statistics, quotes, sources, testimonials or achievements.
- Text or key information unreadable (contrast under AA, too small, clipped, overlapping).
- Core function broken (dead primary link, unusable form, layout broken on mobile).
- Content misleading (truncated axis, distorted data, contradictory claims).
- Required constraints violated (competition rules, word limits, language, brand).
- Assistant residue or placeholder text left in the deliverable.
- Plagiarised or uncredited material.
Aesthetics cannot offset these.

## 4. Bands
90-100 genuinely excellent: specific concept, craft visible, no critical failure, evidence recorded for each 9+.
80-89 strong, professional, small refinements remain.
70-79 competent but partly generic.
50-69 visibly flawed or templated.
Below 50 needs rebuild.
A 90+ needs a written justification of why each dimension earns 9 or more, and an honest list of remaining weaknesses.

## 5. How to score
1. Inspect the real artifact (render it, view it, run it, read it aloud). Do not score from the code or draft alone.
2. For each dimension, cite one concrete observation.
3. Mark critical failures first.
4. Sum, apply caps, give the band.
5. Say it is a structured judgment, not a measurement. Calibrate against examples/stress-tests.md.

## 6. Self-critique procedure
Ask in order: What is the single point? Where does the eye go first? What would a hostile critic point at? Which element is there only because it was the first idea? What did I assume that the user never said? What breaks on a phone, in grayscale, at 200% zoom, in print, in another language? Which claims have no source?

## 7. Revision loop
Fix critical failures, then hierarchy, then clarity, then consistency, then craft. Re-score. Stop when two consecutive passes change little, or the user's constraints are met. Do not keep adding.
