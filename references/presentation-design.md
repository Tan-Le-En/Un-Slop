# Presentation design

Contents: 1 failures, 2 structure, 3 slide design, 4 data and diagrams, 5 delivery notes.

## 1. Failures
Template-default title and bullet slides; topic titles ("Overview"); paragraphs on slides; decorative stock images; different layouts per slide; clip-art icons; unreadable charts; every slide the same density; final slide "Thank you" with nothing else.

## 2. Structure
- Start from the audience question and the decision or feeling wanted at the end.
- Story spine: situation, tension, evidence, resolution, ask. Cut any slide that does not move it.
- Slide count follows time and content, not a number. About one idea per minute is a rough guide.
- Headline = the claim (full sentence or short assertion). Body = evidence that proves it.

## 3. Slide design
- One master: fixed margins, a title position, a type scale, a palette with roles. Reuse 3-5 layouts.
- Minimum body size 24pt for projected rooms; 18pt only for handouts.
- One focal point per slide. Remove logos, footers and decoration that repeat without use.
- Full-bleed relevant images with a scrim for text, or no image. Never a stock handshake.
- Build reveals only when they sequence an argument.
- Keep contrast high for projectors (dark on light is safer in bright rooms).

## 4. Data and diagrams
Apply poster-design.md section 6. Simplify diagrams to the minimum shapes; align and distribute; consistent arrows; label directly. Show the "before and after" or "this versus that" side by side.

## 5. Delivery notes
Notes carry what is said; slides carry what must be seen. Provide a handout or appendix for detail rather than crowding slides.
